Think Before You Click!

An Online Behaviour Challenge

Sort all 20 examples of online behaviour into the correct categories. You can drag a card, or tap a card and then tap a category. Click a category heading to learn more about it.

🖐 Examples to sort (20 cards)

📱 Cyberbullying 🚫

Repeated behaviour using digital technology to hurt, embarrass, threaten or exclude someone.

❤️ Kind online behaviour 👍

Using technology to be friendly, supportive, encouraging and respectful.

😟 Unkind online behaviour 👎

A hurtful or disrespectful online action. It may happen once rather than being repeated, but it is still not okay.

🛡️ Safe online choices 🔒

Actions that protect ourselves or others, including asking for help, reporting behaviour and protecting personal information.

Choose an example to get started!
